About the Project

The Female Epistemology in STEM project explores the ways in which women experience, interpret, and contribute to knowledge within science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. While STEM fields often emphasize objectivity and universality, the lived experiences of scientists shape how questions are asked, problems are approached, and discoveries are interpreted. Through poetry, personal narratives, and visual media, this project reflects on the intellectual and emotional landscapes navigated by women in scientific spaces. By blending creative writing with scientific reflection, we hope to create a space that acknowledges diverse perspectives in knowledge creation and encourages dialogue about representation, identity, and belonging in STEM.
